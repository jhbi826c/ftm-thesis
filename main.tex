\chapter{About this Template}
\label{chap:about}
Everything this thesis needs is in three files: \texttt{main.tex}, the class \texttt{ftmthesis.cls} and the bibliography \texttt{references.bib}.
Images go into \texttt{figs/}.
Delete this chapter once you have read it.

\section{Language}
Write \texttt{lang=en} or \texttt{lang=de} in the first line, whichever language you write in:
\begin{lstlisting}[language={[LaTeX]TeX}]
\documentclass[lang=de]{ftmthesis}
\end{lstlisting}
Add further options after it if you need them, for example \texttt{BCOR=10mm} for a wider binding correction or \texttt{oneside} if the thesis is not printed double-sided.

Use \verb|\iflang{<german>}{<english>}| wherever a piece of text has to differ between the two languages.

\section{Cover Page}
Fill in the commands above \verb|\maketitle|: \verb|\title|, \verb|\author|, \verb|\date|, \verb|\dateofbirth|, \verb|\placeofbirth|, \verb|\matriculationnumber|, \verb|\supervisor| and \verb|\referee|.
Separate two referees with \verb|\and|.

State the type of work with \verb|\thesis| and \verb|\graduation|:
\begin{lstlisting}[language={[LaTeX]TeX}]
\thesis{diploma}
\graduation[Dipl.-Ing.]{Diplomingenieur}
\end{lstlisting}
Instead of \texttt{diploma} you can write \texttt{bachelor}, \texttt{master}, \texttt{student}, \texttt{project} or \texttt{seminar}; the full list is in the \texttt{tudscr} documentation.

Faculty, chair and the FTM logo are already set, leave them alone.

\section{Front Matter}
Leave the \texttt{declarations} environment as it is, the statement of authorship needs no input.

Write the abstract twice, German first, English second:
\begin{lstlisting}[language={[LaTeX]TeX}]
\makeabstract
{Das ist eine Zusammenfassung.}
{This is an abstract.}
\end{lstlisting}

Keep or delete \verb|\tableofcontents|, \verb|\listoffigures| and \verb|\listoftables| as you need them.

Declare every abbreviation above \verb|\makeacronyms| and write \verb|\gls{edx}| in the text:
\begin{lstlisting}[language={[LaTeX]TeX}]
\newacronym{edx}{EDX}{Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy}
\end{lstlisting}
The first use spells it out, \gls{edx}, every use after that is short, \gls{edx}.
An abbreviation you never use does not appear in the list.

\section{Main Matter}
Write your chapters after \verb|\mainmatter| with \verb|\chapter| and \verb|\section|.
Give anything you want to point at a \verb|\label| and refer to it with \verb|\Cref|, which supplies the name itself, as in \Cref{chap:about}.

\section{Figures and Tables}
Put your images into \texttt{figs/} and scale them relative to \verb|\linewidth|, see \Cref{fig:logo}.
Write the \verb|\caption| first and the \verb|\label| after it.

\begin{figure}[htb]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[width=0.45\linewidth]{figs/ftm-logo.pdf}
	\caption{A figure with a caption below it.}
	\label{fig:logo}
\end{figure}

Build tables with \verb|\toprule|, \verb|\midrule| and \verb|\bottomrule|, without vertical rules, see \Cref{tab:example}.
Put the caption above the table.

\begin{table}[htb]
	\centering
	\caption{A table with its caption above it.}
	\label{tab:example}
	\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
		\toprule
		Title 1     & Title 2     & Title 3     \\ \midrule
		Element 1.1 & Element 1.2 & Element 1.3 \\
		Element 2.1 & Element 2.2 & Element 2.3 \\
		Element 3.1 & Element 3.2 & Element 3.3 \\ \bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Write displayed equations in an \texttt{equation} environment and use \verb|\bm| for bold symbols:
\begin{equation}
	E^{2} = \left(\bm{p}\,c\right)^{2} + \left(m_{0}\,c^{2}\right)^{2},
	\qquad
	\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \bm{v}^{2}/c^{2}}}
	\label{eq:relativity}
\end{equation}
\Cref{eq:relativity} relates the energy of a particle to its momentum and its rest mass.

\section{Bibliography}
Paste the BibTeX entry offered by the publisher or the search engine into \texttt{references.bib} and cite it by its key: \verb|\cite{mathiszik2024non}| gives \cite{mathiszik2024non}.
An entry you never cite is not printed.
